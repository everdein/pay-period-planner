# Shared visual system

<!-- cspell:ignore everdein -->

The portfolio, Pay Period Planner and DSA Dojo share a system-font pairing:

- Interface text: Arial, Helvetica and system sans-serif fallbacks.
- Display headings: bold Arial with the same sans-serif fallbacks.
- Code: Consolas, Courier New and monospace fallbacks.

Fonts are supplied by the device; no font service or remote font request is required.

Dark Modern uses #1F1F1F for the canvas, #181818 for side panels, #CCCCCC for body text, #0078D4 for actions and focus, and #9CDCFE for readable blue accents. Yellow #DCDCAA, magenta #C586C0 and salmon #CE9178 distinguish supporting details. Error states use #F85149. Interface actions remain blue; the personal MC mark includes a small teal #4EC9B0 terminal. Light mode, where supported, keeps neutral surfaces and blue interactions.

Application screenshots are real browser captures. Planner captures use an isolated synthetic workspace. Marketing thumbnails are illustrative and are refreshed when the visible design changes.

Large panels stay neutral charcoal. Light blue, magenta, warm yellow and salmon distinguish labels and supporting details, matching the portfolio technology labels. Blue marks actions and focus rather than filling decorative panels.

## Personal identity and media

The approved Syntax Accent identity uses one geometric MC mark for Matthew
Clark, with @everdein as the secondary handle. The main mark is neutral, with
one pink #C586C0 pixel, a light-blue #9CDCFE M terminal, and a teal #4EC9B0 C
terminal. Dark lockups use a white name and warm-yellow #DCDCAA handle.
Dojo retains Pip and its own product icon; MC indicates authorship.

Thumbnails share Arial Bold, white two-line titles, light-blue first supporting
lines, warm-yellow second supporting lines, a single destination-specific call
to action, and a small integrated MC signature. Artwork uses a coordinated
three-quarter view and soft upper-left lighting. It is illustrative marketing,
separate from real application screenshots and synthetic evidence captures.

The approved masters, exports, and usage guide are in the
[Brand Library](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/124_uxAkQmThnHkAbvG4J7vz3TeCKAD9i).
Refresh the appropriate live-product or source-repository variant, preserving
its destination. Do not replace application evidence screenshots with artwork.
